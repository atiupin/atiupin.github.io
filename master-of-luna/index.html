Master of Luna

A turn-based 4X strategy game where you take the role of a half-forgotten deity seeking to regain their former power. It features powerful magic, tactical battles, city building, sweet pixel art, and a calm melancholic atmosphere.

Description

Play as a sleeper, a dormant deity that time left behind. Reclaim what was yours and ascend once again.

On the world map, send your armies to explore, capture neutral towns, and plunder monster lairs. Use the resources you find to advance your economy and then recruit or summon even stronger creatures. At the same time, inscribe new spells to eventually gain access to the most powerful magic.

When armies meet, fight it out in tactical combat: cast your hero's spells and use your creatures' special attacks and abilities.

Features

Exploration. A misty world full of resources, spells, and unexpected allies.

City building. Towns that grow on a careful balance of gold, mana, and rare resources.

Tactical combat. Battles won by maneuvering, combat spells, and creatures' special abilities.

A respectful attitude. Few decisions, but each one carries weight. No micromanagement. Instant turns.

A melancholic vibe. Handmade pixel art, eternal twilight, and a calm and unhurried mood.
